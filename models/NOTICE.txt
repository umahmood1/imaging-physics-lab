densenet121-res224-nih.onnx

A chest X-ray classifier: DenseNet121 trained on the NIH ChestX-ray14 dataset, from the
TorchXRayVision library (https://github.com/mlmed/torchxrayvision), weights "densenet121-res224-nih".

License: Apache License 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).
Citation: Cohen JP, Viviano JD, Bertin P, et al. TorchXRayVision: A library of chest X-ray datasets
and models. Medical Imaging with Deep Learning, 2022. https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.00595

This file holds the published weights in ONNX form. Its numbers were compared with the official
PyTorch file: every tensor is either identical or equal after merging a convolution with the
batch normalization that follows it.

For teaching only. Not a medical device, and not for clinical use.
