Projection geometry

The geometry of a radiograph: source, object, detector, and the signal between them.

Parallel beam

A beam of parallel rays on a detector with two regions: how much of the beam each region receives.

Try this: Slide the beam to the center and watch the two areas become equal.

Computed tomography (CT)

Many views around the patient, combined into one cross-section.

Start here

CT scanner

A working model of a CT scanner, from the X-ray tube to the image.

Try this: Remove one part, such as the bowtie filter, and see what it does to the image.

5 minutes

CT artifacts

Eight CT artifacts in a chest image, each with its cause, its physics, and its fix.

Try this: Make one detector read a little low and find the ring in the patient.

10 minutes

CT reconstruction

How one ray becomes a sinogram, and how a sinogram becomes an image.

Try this: Spin the scanner and watch the sinogram build one row at a time.
