How much of the beam gets through the block?

A block 10 cm deep sits in the middle of the beam, 45 cm from the source. Its attenuation coefficient is μ = 0.050 cm⁻¹. Find the signal at the center of the detector as a fraction of the signal with no block in the beam.

The picture shows this setup from the side. The ray to the center of the detector runs straight through the block; the red part is the length L it travels inside.

Why find the path length first? A ray loses signal only inside material, and how much it loses depends on how far it travels there:

So stage 1 finds L and stage 2 puts it into the exponential. On the central ray the distance and angle factors are both 1, so nothing else changes the signal.
