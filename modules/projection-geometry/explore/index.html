Projection Geometry and Detector Signal

Introduction. This is a model of how a radiograph forms: an X-ray source, a collimator, an object, and a flat detector. Use it to see why one point on the detector receives more or less signal than another.

How to use this page

  1. Pick a point. Click the detector in any picture. The point, P, is marked in all three.
  2. Read the result. The signal at P is above the graph. The length of material its ray crosses is under the ray picture.
  3. Change the setup. Drag the source, the object, or the detector, or use the panel on the left.
  4. Choose the physics. Idealized is the textbook’s model. Realistic adds the tube’s spectrum, the detector, noise, blur, and scatter.

Calculation and limits

Source to skin = SOD − thickness/2 = 149.0 cm. Incident air kerma = tube output × mAs × a factor for the tube voltage, (kVp/120)² × any change to the beam set in the comparison panel × (100 cm / source-to-skin distance)². Entrance surface air kerma includes backscatter. Detector air kerma uses inverse square to SID and exp(−μ × thickness). DI = 10 log₁₀(detector kerma / target).

The 22 cm thickness, 0.14 cm⁻¹ effective attenuation, backscatter factor, and receptor targets are editable teaching assumptions, not measured case values or clinical recommendations. The tube output, 0.143 mGy/mAs, is a calculated value (SpekPy: tungsten anode at 12°, 120 kVp, 3.0 mm Al, 1 m); replace it with your tube’s measured output. A PA or AP image alone does not establish patient thickness; no paired calibrated lateral view is available for these cases. The homogeneous path estimate omits the distinct lung and bone paths, scatter at the receptor, automatic exposure control, and measured beam calibration. The MTF estimate combines a Gaussian blur with a square-pixel aperture.

Sources: AAPM Task Group 116 (the deviation index); IAEA, Diagnostic Radiology Physics.

Regional score sensitivity

Where does covering part of the image change the score for Cardiomegaly? The image is divided into 7 × 7 squares. Each square in turn is covered with the image’s average gray and the model is run again: 49 runs for A and 49 for B, which takes about half a minute.
